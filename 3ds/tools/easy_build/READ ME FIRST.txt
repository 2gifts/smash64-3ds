Smash 64 for New 3DS - CIA builder
==================================

This makes Smash 64 for New 3DS, including CIA files to install on your 3DS,
from YOUR OWN copy of Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 64. Nothing from the
game is included in this download.

What you need
-------------
- A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) with an internet connection
- About 3 GB of free disk space
- Your Super Smash Bros. ROM: the US version (revision 1.0), 16 MB.
  .z64, .n64 and .v64 files all work. If it is in a .zip or .7z, extract it.
- A New 3DS, New 3DS XL or New 2DS XL with custom firmware and FBI

How to build
------------
1. Extract this whole zip first (right-click it > Extract All...).
2. Double-click "Build Smash 64 CIA.bat" (or drag your ROM onto it).
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
   "Run anyway".
3. Choose your ROM when asked, then wait. The first build takes 10 to 30
   minutes. Leave the window open.
4. When it finishes, a folder opens with your files. Follow
   "What to do next.txt" in that folder.

You get two CIA files and can install one or both. They keep separate saves:
  Smash64-New3DS.cia           Smash 64 (unlock things by playing)
  Smash64-New3DS-Unlocked.cia  Smash 64: All Unlocked

If the build stops, run it again: it continues where it stopped.

The builder works in C:\Smash64Build (or another drive with enough space;
the window shows where). You can delete that folder once your files are on
the SD card, or keep it to make future updates faster.
